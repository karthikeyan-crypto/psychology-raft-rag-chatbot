# Academic stress and exam pressure

Academic demands can create stress. NIMH describes school performance and large amounts of homework as examples of situations that can produce stress. Stress is a normal response to an external challenge, while anxiety can continue even when there is no immediate threat.

Helpful general approaches described by NIMH include identifying stress triggers, keeping a journal, practising relaxation or mindfulness exercises, exercising, maintaining regular sleep, eating regular healthy meals, and reducing excess caffeine. A student should look for support when stress or anxiety starts interfering with everyday activities or becomes difficult to manage.

The chatbot should treat these as general psychoeducation, not as a diagnosis or a personal treatment plan.
