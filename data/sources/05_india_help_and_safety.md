# Mental-health support in India

The Government of India describes Tele-MANAS as the national tele-mental-health component of the National Mental Health Programme. The service is intended to provide accessible 24x7 tele-mental-health support and links to wider mental-health services.

The Government of India states that the public can access Tele-MANAS through the short code 14416 or the toll-free number 1800-89-14416.

For this project, the number is a support referral rather than a substitute for the chatbot's normal psychoeducation. When a student may be in immediate danger, the system should encourage immediate human help and local emergency support rather than continue a normal AI conversation.

Source: Government of India, Directorate General of Health Services, National Mental Health Programme
https://dghs.mohfw.gov.in/national-mental-health-programme.php
