# Sleep and healthy routines

Sleep is an important part of mental and physical well-being. WHO explains that getting enough sleep can help the body and mind recover from stress. Its general sleep-hygiene guidance includes keeping a consistent sleep schedule, making the sleeping environment comfortable, limiting electronic-device use before sleep, avoiding large meals and caffeine close to bedtime, and being physically active during the day.

NIMH also lists sleep, regular physical activity, healthy meals, hydration, relaxing activities, and realistic priorities as parts of caring for mental health.

For a student chatbot, these ideas can be presented as general habits to consider. They should not be presented as a guaranteed treatment for a mental-health disorder.
