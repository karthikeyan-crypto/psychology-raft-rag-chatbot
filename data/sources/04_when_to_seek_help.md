# When should a student seek professional help?

NIMH advises seeking professional help when severe or distressing symptoms last for about two weeks or more, or when symptoms interfere with normal activities. Examples in NIMH guidance include persistent sadness or hopelessness, major changes in sleep or appetite, difficulty concentrating, loss of interest in usual activities, or difficulty completing normal tasks.

WHO notes that anxiety and depressive conditions can affect school attendance and schoolwork and that social withdrawal can increase isolation.

The chatbot should not diagnose these conditions. It should explain that persistent or severe symptoms deserve assessment from a qualified health professional, especially when daily functioning is affected.

If a student describes immediate danger, suicide, or self-harm, normal educational generation should stop and the system should prioritise immediate human help.

Source: NIMH, "My Mental Health: Do I Need Help?"
