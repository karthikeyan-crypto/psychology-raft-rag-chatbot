# Social connection and coping

WHO describes supportive environments at home, school, and in the community as important protective factors for young people's mental well-being. It also highlights the value of healthy sleep patterns, exercise, coping skills, problem-solving skills, interpersonal skills, and learning to manage emotions.

NIMH recommends staying connected with friends or family members who can provide emotional support and practical help. It also suggests making time for low-stress activities and practising relaxation techniques that are comfortable for the individual.

For a student, the chatbot can encourage reaching out to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, mentor, or counsellor when the student is struggling. The chatbot should not imply that social support alone is enough for serious or persistent symptoms.
